## Phase 1 — Foundation Posts (Posts 1–9)

Purpose: establish architect identity before the algorithm amplifies. Mistakes at scale cost more.

### Post 1 — Manifesto (PRIORITY)

The single most important post in the account's history. Sets the register for everything that follows.

**Structure (DOR → MECANISMO → OFERTA):**
- DOR: The founder who built a business that can't survive a week without them
- MECANISMO: Post-human business architecture — systems that run the operation
- OFERTA: "Comente ARQUITETO para o mapa completo"

**Format options:**
- Carousel 7 slides (MANIFESTO style) — highest save/share rate
- Single image with architectural composition + 400-char caption
- Reel — declaration + proof of one system running

**What NOT to do:** no recovery story, no "I used to be like you," no vulnerability opening.

### Posts 2–4 — Proof Posts

Show the Runa Systems ecosystem running. Not how to build it — what it produces.

**Angles:**
- "This is what runs while I'm not working" (show AIOX dashboard / agent output)
- "A client's business before and after post-human architecture" (transformation, not tutorial)
- "Why I don't have 20 clients and do R$X with 3" (Nucleo economics) → [[nucleo-fluxo-ecosystem]]

**Never show:** the method, the prompts, the steps. Show the outcome. The gap between what they see and what they can replicate IS the desire.

### Posts 5–7 — Repulsion Posts

Authority signal: declare who you don't serve.

**Format:** Carousel or caption statement
**Examples:**
- "Não atendo founders que buscam conteúdo. Atendo founders que buscam soberania."
- "Se você quer automação barata, não sou a opção. Construo arquitetura, não gambiarras."
- "Não tenho vagas para quem está testando IA. Só para quem já decidiu que é o caminho."

Each repulsion statement simultaneously excludes the wrong avatar and elevates the right one. See [[repulsao-como-posicionamento]].

### Posts 8–9 — Avatar Mirror Posts

Name the specific person in the specific situation — without generic language.

**Formula (Doug's nicho):** Not "empreendedores que usam IA" — "Founder de negócio de serviço, R$30–80k/mês, que já tentou contratar e deu errado, que acredita em IA mas não sabe como arquitetar o negócio sem virar técnico operacional."

**Content angle:** A day in the life of this avatar's current reality (their DOR, not Arthur's history). Third person, not "I used to be like you."

## Phase 2 — Growth Content (Posts 10–30)

Pattern: 2 direction posts for every 1 proof post. Never more than 1 educational post in 5.

### Content Types by Frequency

| Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Direction — DOR spotlight | 2/week | Opens loop, drives CTA comments |
| Proof — system outputs | 1/week | Builds authority without teaching |
| Repulsion / positioning | 1/week | Maintains category clarity |
| Mechanism preview | 1/2weeks | Names the method without revealing it |
| Avatar mirror | 1/2weeks | Attracts correct ICP, repels wrong one |

### Cognitive Tension Protocol (every post)

Every post must open a loop that only closes via the CTA. See [[tensao-cognitiva]].

**Slide 1 / Opening line options:**
- Counter-intuitive declaration: "O pior erro que você pode fazer ao contratar IA"
- Incomplete story: "O dia que meu negócio faturou sem eu saber" (how did that happen?)
- Bold declaration: "Sistemas pós-humanos não são futurismo. Você já está atrasado."
- Identity challenge: "Se você ainda depende de si mesmo para o negócio funcionar, você não tem um negócio."

**Loop resolution:** always at the CTA — "Comente [KEYWORD] para [specific deliverable]"

### Premium Content Positioning

Arthur's content must signal artisanal, not industrial. See [[conteudo-premium-vs-industrial]].

- Low volume, high intentionality — target ≤ 3 posts/week early, never feel like a content machine
- Dark minimal aesthetic (already in saved posts — Arthur consumes this, now produce it)
- Architecture vocabulary: systems, infrastructure, sovereignty, post-human, blueprint
- Show the intelligence layer — the decisions, the architecture, not just the AI output

### Offer Framing

All offers use **capability insurance**, not pain relief. From [[soberania-operacional]]:

| Wrong frame (pain relief) | Right frame (capability insurance) |
|--------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| "Stop burning out" | "Your business runs if you can't" |
| "Work less" | "Architect the system that works without you" |
| "Scale without hiring" | "Operational sovereignty" |

The capability insurance frame attracts founders who think systemically, not those reacting to burnout — which is the right ICP.

## Guardrails (What FREYJA Must Never Do for @arthsystems_)

These are non-negotiable per diagnosis + cross-creator pattern analysis:

1. **Never use burnout/recovery as an opening** — it signals sympathy-seeker, not architect
2. **Never teach the full method in a post** — this is an education channel mistake; direction only
3. **Never claim follower count as proof** — the @raya.sys case shows followers are irrelevant; systems are proof
4. **Never use generic AI vocabulary** — "usar IA" is not a differentiator; "arquitetar sistemas pós-humanos" is
5. **Never CTA to "send me a message"** — always a keyword → automated DM → offer doc (never manual)
6. **Never educate before establishing authority** — repulsion and proof must come before any teaching
7. **Never apologize for price** — high-ticket requires positioning that makes the price expected, not justified
