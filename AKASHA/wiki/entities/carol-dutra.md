# Carol Dutra

Brazilian creator (@acaroldutraa) — studied as extreme example of minimalist high-ticket positioning. Runs a complete conversion machine with only 10 posts.

## Key Metrics (as of 2026-03-18)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Handle | @acaroldutraa |
| Followers | 3,200 |
| Posts | 10 |
| Revenue | Not publicly disclosed |
| Keyword | SILENCIOSO (ManyChat trigger) |

## Core Positioning: Marketing Silencioso

Carol's central thesis — and the product she sells — is that high-ticket sales can happen with minimal public presence, zero loud promotion, and maximum selectivity. → [[marketing-silencioso]]

Her own account IS the proof of concept: 3.2k followers, 10 posts, complete sales system.

## Funnel Architecture (FLP)

Carol operates a **Funil Low Profile** (FLP) → [[flp-funil-low-profile]]

```
9-slide carousel (qualifier)
  └─► CTA: "comente SILENCIOSO"
       └─► ManyChat DM automation fires
            └─► Link to WhatsApp or Offer Page
                 └─► Close via conversation
```

## Selective Repulsion

Hard public exclusion statement: "Não trabalho com quem está começando."

This serves three functions:
1. Filters out non-ICP leads before they enter the funnel
2. Creates aspiration — beginners want to qualify to work with her
3. Signals premium positioning without price declaration → [[repulsao-como-posicionamento]]

## Carousel Template (9-slide structure)

Carol's carousel follows a consistent template that functions as qualifier + proof + CTA in one piece:

| Slide | Function |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Hook — bold claim or pattern interrupt |
| 2–4 | Problem articulation (ICP recognition) |
| 5–7 | Mechanism introduction (why this works) |
| 8 | Social proof or result |
| 9 | CTA + keyword instruction |

## Key Insight

**1 concept, 1 CTA, silence**: Carol proves that volume of content is not correlated with conversion. One strong concept, executed repeatedly with one clear action, outperforms high-frequency noise.

The silence (few posts, low follower count) is itself a positioning signal: "I am selective. My clients are selected, not attracted."
