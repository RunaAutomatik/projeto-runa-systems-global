## Core Positioning

- Instagram is a **direction channel**, not an education channel → [[canal-direcao-vs-educacao]]
- Every caption is an **invisible sales letter** — private conversation disguised as public content → [[carta-vendas-invisivel]]
- Uses **cognitive tension** to hold attention: pattern interruption + unresolved question → [[tensao-cognitiva]]
- Proof model: lifestyle as evidence (Porsche, Disney, Florida) — never explicitly declares wealth; the life demonstrates it

## Master Content Formula

Every post follows: **DOR → MECANISMO → OFERTA** → [[dor-mecanismo-oferta]]

> "A narrativa é o fio condutor que faz o cara escorregar da dor até o pix sem perceber que foi conduzido."

## Product Ecosystem

- **Núcleo** (R$15k+ — 100% margin): full transformation program → [[nucleo-fluxo-ecosystem]]
- **Fluxo** (R$97–297): specific fast-win products that build trust + pay costs
- Evidence: @raya.sys — first 45 days: R$135k; December (no posting): R$27k (9 × R$3k) with R$100-150/day paid traffic

## Funnel Architecture

Stories Ads → profile → bio doc → DM → close (high-ticket)

**Critical insight:** The bio doc (LP) converts, not content. Content drives traffic. A ready profile + R$100/day traffic = sales machine without constant content.
