## Core Positioning

- **Persona built above the person**: "Sarah Seller" is a declared character, deliberately separated from "Sarah the human" — this creates a brand entity with consistent behavior and aesthetic
- **Science as differentiator**: Uses neuroscience and internal mechanics as content vocabulary, elevating above generic mindset/coaching market
- **Philosophical density**: Content is not inspirational — it is analytical and cognitively demanding, which repels casual followers and attracts committed buyers → [[repulsao-como-posicionamento]]

## Visual Identity

- **Preto absoluto** (absolute black): not an aesthetic choice — a philosophical statement. Black = void = infinite potential. Consistent across all content.
- Zero decoration; high intentionality per pixel
- Contrast: white text on black only

## Core Concept: Internal Mechanics

> "Faturamento é sintoma. A cura está na mecânica interna."

Sarah's central thesis: revenue is a symptom of internal cognitive and psychological mechanics. Fixing the surface metric (revenue) without addressing internal patterns is ineffective. → [[mecanica-interna]]

Applied to sales: high-ticket sales resistance often isn't about money — it's about internal narratives (fear of visibility, imposter syndrome, scarcity programming).

## Content Pattern

- **Low post count, high intentionality**: 36 posts, each functioning as a standalone philosophical argument
- **No burnout narrative**: pure architect positioning — competence, not vulnerability
- **Science citations**: grounds claims in neuroscience literature, not personal opinion
