# Instagram Market Analysis — March 2026

Four-profile study conducted 2026-03-18. Purpose: extract narrative patterns and positioning benchmarks from high-performing Brazilian high-ticket creators for application to @arthsystems_.

## Cross-Profile Patterns

**What high-performing profiles share:**
- Hard niche + hard exclusion ("I don't work with X")
- Low post count, high intentionality (10–37 posts)
- Authority through repulsion, not attraction → [[repulsao-como-posicionamento]]
- Offer in bio or one click away
- Zero burnout/recovery narrative — pure architect positioning

## Key Contrast: Arthur vs References

Arthur's 5 posts = burnout/recovery arc
Reference profiles = zero vulnerability, pure architect/authority energy

Required shift: recovery → architect → [[arthur-runa]]

## Extractions by Creator

- **Doug D'Marco** → [[doug-demarco]]: canal de direção, carta de vendas invisível, tensão cognitiva

- **Carol Dutra** → [[carol-dutra]]: marketing silencioso, FLP, selective repulsion
