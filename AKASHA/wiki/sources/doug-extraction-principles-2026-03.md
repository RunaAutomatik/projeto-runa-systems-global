## Agency Survival Formula (deni.next)

Three requirements for agencies/consultants in 2026:
1. One client type + one specific pain + one specific context
2. Named mechanism (system with name, logic, clear promise — only you sell)
3. Productization (beginning, middle, end — not open-ended retainer)

Runa Systems checks all three.
