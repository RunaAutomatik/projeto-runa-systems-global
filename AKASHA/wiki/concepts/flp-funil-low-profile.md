# FLP — Funil Low Profile

Conversion architecture from [[carol-dutra]]: a complete sales funnel built on minimal content, a qualifying carousel, and direct DM automation.

## Architecture

```
1. QUALIFICADOR (Carousel — 9 slides)
   └─► Final CTA: "Comente SILENCIOSO"

2. TRIGGER (ManyChat automation)
   └─► Comment detected → DM fires immediately
        └─► Message: "Vi que você comentou SILENCIOSO..."

3. DM DELIVERY
   └─► WhatsApp link OR offer page URL

4. CLOSE
   └─► Conversation on WhatsApp / landing page checkout
```

## Why Low Profile Works

The funnel requires **zero active selling by the creator**:
- No DM outreach
- No sales calls initiated by the creator
- No constant posting to maintain algorithm visibility
- The carousel runs indefinitely, collecting leads

The creator only appears when a lead has already self-qualified by taking the friction step (commenting).

## Carousel as Qualifier

The 9-slide carousel serves as simultaneous proof + filter:
- Someone who reads all 9 slides and comments the keyword is **pre-committed**
- Someone who isn't the ICP usually stops reading before slide 9
- The content itself is the qualification mechanism

## Comparison to Full-Scale Funnels

| FLP | Traditional Funnel |
|-----|------------------|
| 1 carousel | Multiple landing pages |
| ManyChat (1 automation) | Email sequences + retargeting |
| WhatsApp close | Sales call booking |
| 1 keyword | Multiple lead magnets |
| Creator posts rarely | Content machine required |
