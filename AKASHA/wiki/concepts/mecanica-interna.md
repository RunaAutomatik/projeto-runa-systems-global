# Mecânica Interna

Core concept from [[sarah-seller]]: revenue is a symptom of internal cognitive and psychological mechanics. Fixing surface metrics without addressing internal patterns is ineffective.

## The Thesis

> "Faturamento é sintoma. A cura está na mecânica interna."

What most coaches, consultants, and programs address:
- Revenue (the symptom)
- Strategy and tactics
- External systems and processes

What Sarah addresses:
- The internal psychological mechanics that determine whether a person can execute strategy
- Cognitive patterns that create invisible ceilings on income
- Neurological programming that drives decision-making in sales contexts

## Application in Sales Context

In high-ticket sales, resistance often has nothing to do with money. The mechanics driving objections:
- Fear of visibility (visibility = judgment risk)
- Imposter syndrome (the internal verdict: "I'm not good enough to charge this")
- Scarcity programming (money = rare; charging = losing relationships)
- Autonomy conflict (being paid = being controlled)

Understanding the buyer's internal mechanics allows the seller to address the **real** objection instead of the stated one.

## Neuroscience as Differentiator

Sarah uses neuroscience vocabulary (not pop psychology) as a positioning tool. This serves two functions:
1. **Elevates above generic "mindset" content** — science citations vs opinion
2. **Pre-qualifies ICP** — buyers who engage with neuroscience-based content are more intellectually committed and tend to pay more with less friction

## Connection to Cognitive Tension

Internal mechanics determine whether [[tensao-cognitiva]] resolves into action or anxiety. When a prospect has scarcity programming or imposter syndrome, the open loop created by cognitive tension content closes as paralysis ("I can't afford it / I'm not ready") rather than purchase. High-ticket content must address the internal mechanics to make the tension convert. This is why Sarah's AKASHA program sells at high-ticket while most "mindset" content has low perceived value — she works the mechanism, not the symptom.

In [[dor-mecanismo-oferta]] terms: the DOR must name the internal pattern, not the external result.
