# Tensão Cognitiva

Copywriting technique from [[doug-demarco]]: deliberately create an unresolved question or pattern interruption in the reader's mind that **compels continued reading** until the tension is resolved.

## How It Works

The brain has a strong drive to resolve open loops (Zeigarnik Effect). Cognitive tension exploits this: open a loop early in the content, make the reader need to resolve it, and the resolution lives at the end — near the CTA.

## Techniques Used by Doug

| Technique | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| Counter-intuitive claim | "Não preciso de mais seguidores para faturar mais" |
| Incomplete story | "Quando fechei aquela venda de 40k às 23h, entendi que tinha errado tudo até ali..." |
| Bold declaration without proof (yet) | "Instagram não é um canal de educação. E quem trata assim está trabalhando de graça." |
| Identity challenge | "Se você ainda mede resultado em curtidas, você ainda não entendeu o jogo." |

## Application in Carousel Format

In a 7-9 slide carousel:
- **Slide 1**: Maximum tension — the pattern interrupt or bold claim
- **Slides 2–6**: Build and sustain tension through evidence and partial resolution
- **Slide 7–8**: Resolution — the mechanism that explains everything
- **Last slide**: CTA — the action that completes the resolution

The reader who reaches the last slide is already pre-sold. They arrived because they **needed** to know the answer.

## Relationship to Other Concepts

Cognitive tension is the mechanism that makes the → [[carta-vendas-invisivel]] work without the reader noticing they are being sold to. It is also the psychological engine behind the DOR phase of → [[dor-mecanismo-oferta]].
