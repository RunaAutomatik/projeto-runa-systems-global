# Repulsão como Posicionamento

Positioning technique used by [[doug-demarco]] and [[carol-dutra]]: declaring publicly what you do NOT do, who you do NOT serve, and what you do NOT sell — as a primary authority signal.

## The Mechanism

In crowded markets, everyone promises they can help everyone. Specificity and exclusion signal that you are selective about who you work with — which implies your clients are also selected, and your standards are high.

The anti-pitch IS the pitch.

## Examples in Practice

**Doug D'Marco:**
> "Do por que não vendo cursinho de influencer de IA... Isso é troco de pinga perto da venda de conhecimento real."

**Carol Dutra:**
> "Não trabalho com quem está começando."

Both statements do three things simultaneously:
1. Filter out non-ICP leads
2. Create aspiration (beginners want to qualify)
3. Imply premium positioning without stating price

## Why It Works Psychologically

- **Exclusivity heuristic**: What is hard to access appears more valuable
- **Confidence signal**: Only someone certain of their value can afford to refuse clients
- **Reverse social proof**: "If they're this selective, their clients must be exceptional"

## Application to @arthsystems_

Arthur's repulsion statement (as identified in analysis):
> "Não construo automações. Não faço consultoria de IA. Construo sistemas operacionais para negócios."

The repulsion defines the category. By saying what Arthur is NOT (automation vendor, generic AI consultant), the positioning of what Arthur IS (systems architect) becomes sharper.

## Repulsion as Category Creation

When you declare "I don't do X", you simultaneously imply "I do Y (which is different from and superior to X)." This creates a new category without requiring a lengthy explanation.

Carol's "Marketing Silencioso" is itself a repulsion play — it defines against loud, high-volume, everyone-welcome marketing. → [[marketing-silencioso]]
