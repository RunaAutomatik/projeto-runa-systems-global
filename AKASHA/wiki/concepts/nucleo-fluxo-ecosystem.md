# Núcleo + Fluxo — Product Ecosystem

Product architecture model from [[doug-demarco]]: a two-tier structure where low-ticket products fund the business and build trust, while the high-ticket core program delivers maximum transformation and margin.

## The Model

### Núcleo (Core)
- Price: R$15,000+
- Margin: 100% (time spent = zero in delivery, or minimal)
- Role: main program, real transformation, anchor of authority
- Where all content ultimately points

### Fluxo (Flow)
- Price: R$97–R$297
- Role: resolve specific problems fast; build confidence before Núcleo
- Function: "Pay 100% of business costs" with volume
- Entry point: allows someone who "doesn't have the money" to start building trust and cashflow

> "Se você não tem dinheiro [para o Núcleo]: eles te ajudam a fazer o caixa necessário para o Núcleo"

## Business Math

R$300k–R$1M/month solo → requires a scalable program at R$5,000+

You cannot reach this with R$297 products alone (would need 1,000–3,000 sales/month). The Núcleo is the engine; the Fluxo feeds it.

## Doug's Proof: @raya.sys

- First 45 days: R$135,000 with R$3,000/sale offers
- December (no posting): R$27,000 → 9 sales of R$3,000
- Mechanism: R$100–150/day traffic → bio doc converts

## Applied to Arthur (Runa Systems)

| Layer | Doug | Arthur |
|-------|------|--------|
| Núcleo | Full transformation program R$15k+ | Runa Systems Method (gravado R$15k / intervenção R$50k / mentoria R$30k) |
| Fluxo | Specific agents and tools R$97–297 | CREATOR$, $QUAD, POSICIONAMENTO$, SITE$, AGENTE$ (R$97–297 → R$1.997–3.997) |

```
Post (direction channel)

            └─► Trust + confidence built

```
