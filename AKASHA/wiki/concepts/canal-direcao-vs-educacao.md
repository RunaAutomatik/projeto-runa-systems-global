# Canal de Direção vs Educação

Concept from [[doug-demarco]]: Instagram (and most social media) should be used as a **direction channel**, not an education channel.

## Core Distinction

| Education Channel | Direction Channel |
|------------------|------------------|
| Teaches complete concepts | Points toward a transformation |
| Viewer learns and leaves | Viewer is moved to act |
| Authority through knowledge transfer | Authority through result demonstration |
| Content is the product | Content is the funnel |
| Scales content, not revenue | Scales revenue |

## Why This Matters

Most creators mistake Instagram for a classroom. They post complete tutorials, full frameworks, detailed how-tos — and wonder why no one buys. The follower gets the value from the post and never needs the paid offer.

A direction channel gives **just enough** to:
1. Make the problem vivid (DOR)
2. Show there is a specific mechanism that solves it (MECANISMO)
3. Point clearly to the next step (OFERTA / CTA)

The post is not the answer. It is the **question that makes the answer necessary**.

## Application to @arthsystems_

Each post should leave the reader understanding:
- What kind of problem Arthur solves (post-human business architecture)
- That a specific method exists (Runa Systems Method)
- Where to take one step forward (comment keyword → DM → offer)

Never teach the system in a post. Show the result of the system. Let the gap between what they see and what they have create the desire to buy.
