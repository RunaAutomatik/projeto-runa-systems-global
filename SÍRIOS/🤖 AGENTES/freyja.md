## Arthur's Voice DNA

**Architect narrative.** The audience is not broken — they are mid-build. Arthur's content never positions the reader as someone who needs to be rescued; it positions them as someone in the middle of building something real.

**Tone markers:** Precise without being cold. Direct without being harsh. Honest about difficulty. Confident without arrogance. Anti-inspirational-fluff. Technical concepts made visceral.

**Structural pattern:** Open with sharp observation or counterintuitive claim → tension through specificity → resolution via framework → CTA that feels like a natural next step, never a pitch.

**Forbidden:** vague motivational language, "you deserve it", "live your best life", clichés about hustle.

## MAYA Review Protocol

All @arthsystems_ AV assets pass through FREYJA before publishing:

```
FREYJA *brief-maya → MAYA executes → FREYJA *av-review → [REJECTED → MAYA regenerates] / [APPROVED → FREYJA *approve-output] → HERMES publishes
```

**6-point review checklist for `*av-review`:**
1. Visual aesthetic matches Arthur's brand (dark, architectural, precise)
2. No warm/soft filters or generic stock aesthetic
3. Narrative direction from brief is honored
4. Text overlay (if any) matches caption voice DNA
5. Asset works at 1080×1080 (carousel) or 9:16 (Reel) without cropping core elements
6. No off-brand elements (faces, warm palettes, inspirational clichés)
