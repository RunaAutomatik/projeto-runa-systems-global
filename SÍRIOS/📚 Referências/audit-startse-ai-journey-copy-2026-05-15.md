## 2. MECHANISM AUDIT — What Makes It Work

### Mechanism 1 — De-commoditization via Surgical Avatar
**Trigger:** "A ÚNICA... PARA QUEM DECIDE"

- "A ÚNICA" kills comparison before it starts
- "PARA QUEM DECIDE" targets an identity of power, not a skill level
- Reader self-identifies in under 3 seconds and stops scrolling
- **Framework:** Hormozi — De-commoditize or Die. If they can compare you, you've already lost.

---

### Mechanism 2 — Identity-Based Transformation Promise
**Trigger:** "Em 12 meses, você sai de X para Y"

- Concrete timeframe eliminates ambiguity
- Explicit before/after in a single sentence
- Destination = social status ("a referência"), not a technical skill
- **Framework:** Hormozi Value Equation — Dream Outcome × Likelihood. People pay premium for who they want to become, not what they want to learn.

---

### Mechanism 3 — Double Qualification Filter
**Trigger:** "É para você se... / Não é para você se..."

- Reduces objections before they arise
- Creates psychological scarcity ("not everyone gets in")
- FOMO effect on the disqualified list
- Creates immediate validation for the qualified list
- **Framework:** Hormozi — Starving Crowd First. Sell to who already has the active problem and the money.

---

### Mechanism 4 — Price Anchoring via Component Stack
**Trigger:** R$ 72.625 → R$ 11.988

- Shows real market value of each component individually
- Creates ~83% perceived discount without reducing price
- Reader stops comparing to other courses — starts comparing to hiring separate consultants
- **Framework:** Hormozi — Stack The Value. You don't need to be cheap. You need the reader to feel they're stealing (in a good way).

---

### Mechanism 5 — Result-Oriented Guarantee (Not Satisfaction-Based)
**Trigger:** "Se você não criar valor... devolvemos 100%"

- Most guarantees say "if you don't like it" — this says "if it doesn't work"
- Transfers risk to the seller
- Structured checkpoint (6 months) = operational credibility, not vague promise
- "No fine print" = eliminates default distrust toward guarantees
- **Framework:** Hormozi — Risk Reversal. The guarantee is not a cost — it's a sales argument.

---

### Mechanism 6 — Problem Framed as Behavior (Not Generic Pain)
**Trigger:** "Acumular conteúdo gratuito não constrói roadmap."

- Confronts the avatar's behavior without attacking their identity
- Creates shame loop: reader recognizes themselves → feels discomfort → wants to resolve
- Extreme specificity: "accumulate free content" — universally relatable
- **Framework:** RECA (Reconhecimento / Emoção / Comprometimento / Ação) — emotional recognition before logical buy-in.

---

### Mechanism 7 — Tangible Deliverable as Core Product
**Trigger:** Diagnóstico de Maturidade + Roadmap de Adoção

- Sells an artifact (report, plan, document), not a "mentorship"
- Buyer can describe what they're buying to someone else
- Reduces perceived risk: "I have something concrete in my hands"
- **Framework:** Brunson — Value Ladder. Each deliverable is a rung, not a feature dump.

---

### Mechanism 8 — First-Person Identity CTAs
**Trigger:** "Quero começar minha jornada"

- Not "Buy Now" or "Sign Up" — verb of identity ownership
- Reader performs the identity before completing the transaction
- **Framework:** RALOCA — commitment through language ("I want" = already decided).

## 6. KEY COPY PRINCIPLES EXTRACTED

1. **Sell identity, not information** — "a referência de IA" beats "aprenda IA"
2. **Name the behavior, not the pain** — "acumular conteúdo gratuito" beats "frustrado com IA"
3. **Anchor with components** — show what it costs separately before showing the price
4. **Guarantee the result, not satisfaction** — transfers risk, builds trust
5. **Qualify and disqualify simultaneously** — creates exclusivity and FOMO at once
6. **CTAs own the identity** — reader performs the decision before clicking
7. **Tangible deliverables over "access"** — artifacts > content > community
